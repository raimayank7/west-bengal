How the code CBIN0281473 is built

CBIN is the bank part: the letters of Central Bank of India. The fifth character is always the digit 0. 281473 is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 731016790 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 731 is the city (the same three digits that begin the PIN code 731101), 016 is the bank and 790 is the branch.
