How the code DBSS0IN0702 is built

DBSS is the bank part: the letters of Development Bank of Singapore. The fifth character is always the digit 0. IN0702 is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 700641005 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 700 is the city (the same three digits that begin the PIN code 700064), 641 is the bank and 005 is the branch.
