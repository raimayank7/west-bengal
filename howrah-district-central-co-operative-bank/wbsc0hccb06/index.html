How the code WBSC0HCCB06 is built

WBSC is the bank part: these are the letters of West Bengal State Co-operative Bank. Howrah District Central Co-operative Bank sends and receives payments through that bank, so its codes carry those letters. The fifth character is always the digit 0. HCCB06 is the part that names this branch.

The MICR code 700817505 is the number printed along the bottom of this branch's cheques. 700 is the city, 817 is the bank and 505 is the branch.
